Chrono Kitchen — a 3D time-hopping cooking game: route ingredients across fire-pit, hearth and nano-cooker stations to serve orders before the timer runs out.

One coherent kitchen, three eras. Grab raw meat, dough, veg and spice from the pantry, transform them at the right station — the prehistoric fire-pit grill, the medieval hearth oven, the futuristic nano-cooker — stack the finished items on the assembly plate to match a ticket, and serve before it runs out. Orders arrive faster and get harder as the 2:30 shift rushes on; three missed orders end your shift. Beat 300 points to win.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and play with keyboard + mouse (WASD/arrows to move, click or E to use a station, 1–4 to grab from the pantry, Space to serve).
